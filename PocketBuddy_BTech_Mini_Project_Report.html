ABSTRACT

College students face significant hurdles in personal financial management. Limited monthly allowances, the dual circulation of liquid Cash and digital UPI, and frequent shared group expenditures—such as hostel room supplies, shared grocery runs, flat rents, and mess bills—frequently lead to premature budget exhaustion and untracked debts.

PocketBuddy — Student Smart Money Manager is a modern full-stack web application built with React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Express.js, and Firebase Firestore. It provides dual-wallet tracking (Cash vs. UPI), collaborative shared room budgeting with zero-loss integer-paise calculations, a two-step receiver-approved settlement protocol, a confidential PIN-protected notes vault, and AI receipt scanning.

Keywords: Personal Finance, Expense Splitting, Two-Party Settlement, Integer-Paise Arithmetic, React 19, Express.js, Firebase Firestore.
